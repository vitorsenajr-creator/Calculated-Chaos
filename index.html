Wall marker setup & calibration

Mount the 4 markers in a cross around where you'll photograph items — not a rectangle. Only 2 distances have to be right (left↔right and top↔bottom), which is much easier to get accurate than a 4-corner rectangle.
  1. Print 4 copies of the marker (button above).
  2. With a real tape measure, decide your horizontal and vertical spacing (wider for bulky items like dresses) — mark the wall in pencil at those 4 points, in a cross shape like above.
  3. Stick each marker so its crosshair's center gap sits exactly on your pencil mark. Use the small tick above each marker to keep it level.
  4. Enter those same spacing numbers in the fields above and save.
  5. When measuring an item photo, choose 🎯 Wall markers as the calibration method, then tap the center gap of each marker in order.
