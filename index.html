Built around the piece, from the shelf to the profit.

Most tools start with the listing. Calculated Chaos starts with the piece in your hands, so the listing, the label and the money all point back to it.

Every piece has a code, a box and a label.

Each piece gets its own product code and a label you print on the thermal printer, in black.

  1. 1CodeIts own product code
  2. 2BoxShows where it is
  3. 3Wall markerShows where it is
  4. 4TransferMoves it

Boxes and wall markers show where the piece is. A stock transfer moves it, so the piece you are looking for is always where the app says it is.

Photos turn into listings, with less typing.

Shoot a batch of photos first, then catalog them when you have time.

Your photos

The draft, ready to check

The ✨ analysis reads the photos and drafts the title, description, category, color, size and a price. You check every field, then publish to eBay straight from the app.

  • Your own writing rules for titles and descriptions
  • Voice notes can add details to a piece

Turn a sale screenshot into a pick list.

Upload the order screenshots from Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Depop, Vinted or any other marketplace.

Each piece is matched by its product code or by its name, sorted by box, and printed as a checklist with a package number for each order. When it ships, mark it sold with the real price, fees and sales tax.

  • Pieces that do not match are shown with suggestions, never guessed
  • A piece already sold or still listed elsewhere gets a warning

Know your real profit, piece by piece.

Every sale keeps its cost, platform fees, shipping and other costs.

Revenue−Cost of goods sold−Selling costs−Operating expenses=Net profit

Finances shows the month, the sales and the pieces still in stock. Profit & Loss adds your business expenses and your mileage at the IRS rate, and one definition of profit is used on every screen and in every export.

  • Sales by month, with the missing costs flagged
  • Expenses by category, with a CSV export for your accountant

Bring the spreadsheet you already keep.

Import your inventory and past sales from Excel or CSV.

The app suggests which column is which and which words mean sold or new with tags. You see a preview with totals and possible duplicates before anything is saved, and you can undo a whole import.

  • Column names in English, Portuguese and Spanish
  • Up to 10,000 rows per file
