# App shell and Home (index.html)

Scope: the app shell (phone bottom bar, desktop sidebar, slim header, one "+" action) and the Home screen; later screens inherit it. Mode: Operate. Audience and job: the solo reseller on a phone, one-handed, between tasks; small teams on desktop. Task: see today's work and act on it, then move to Catalog, Stock, Finances or Settings. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; navigation decisions N1-N11 in CLAUDE.md; three per-business themes (Parcel default, Tag Week, Concourse), so every color, font and radius is a CSS variable. Memorable moment: the tracking line, each piece and the whole business read like a parcel in transit. Unresolved: exact Appearance options; logo slot behavior.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a dispatch desk, not a dashboard. It opens on the numbers that matter for the chosen period and on grouped exceptions, each with one verb; it refuses the category's grid of KPI tiles, charts and branding above the work.

OWN-WORLD: Parcel. Navy shell (#12285A bar and sidebar), cool white ground (#F4F6F9) with white panels and hairline borders, red (#C8102E) only for urgent states, amber for warnings, green for profit. Archivo throughout; condensed bold numerals for codes and figures; the product code in a bordered label box beside its box.

STORY: The seller opens the app and knows in five seconds what is in stock, what sold, what she earned and what needs her, then taps the one action that fixes the top problem.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 wide: 56px navy header with store name and "by Calculated Chaos", search icon. "Today" heading with a Month / Year / All time segmented control. A 2x2 summary panel (In stock, Sold, Profit, Projected). The pipeline tracking line (Cataloged, Listed, Sold, Shipped with counts). "Needs attention" rows with a count badge, a title, a detail line and a verb link. The "+" floats above the navy bottom bar (Home, Catalog, Stock, Finances, Settings).

FORM: Parcel, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list (thrift color tags, woven garment labels, dry-cleaner claim tickets, consignment rack dividers, postal shipping labels, vintage hang tags, resale marketplace app grammar); seed:007b6ac6cba56f1a. Raises kept from declined challengers: one family at many sizes (bitmap specimen), sold items' photos in grayscale (character goods catalog), color quarantined to state and shell (lexicon plates).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
