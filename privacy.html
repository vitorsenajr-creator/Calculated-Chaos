Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Version 2026-10-24

This policy explains what Calculated Chaos ("we", "us") collects when you use the app at calculatedchaos.app, what we do with it, and who else handles it. Calculated Chaos is run by an individual, not a company. Questions or requests: support@calculatedchaos.app.

Calculated Chaos is offered to sellers in the United States only. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

1. What we collect

Account

  • Your email address, and your password (stored by Firebase Authentication; we never see or keep it in readable form) or your Google sign-in identifier.
  • Your role in a store, whether your email is verified, when you were approved, and which version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted and when.

Your store's content

  • Items you catalog: photos, titles, descriptions, brands, sizes, measurements, product codes, boxes, costs, prices, fees, shipping, sale dates, platforms and buyers' order numbers.
  • Money records: sales, profit, expenses, mileage trips and goals.
  • Store settings: store name, logo, theme, platform fees, label setup and your AI writing instructions.
  • Spreadsheets you import (the file is read in your browser; the column names, a few sample rows and the distinct value words are sent to the AI service to suggest a mapping, see section 3).
  • Screenshots of sales that you upload to the Pick list. They can show a buyer's name, username, address and order details.
  • Voice recordings you make with the narration feature.
  • Your eBay connection, if you make one: the authorization tokens are kept on our server and never sent to your browser.

Activity records

  • AI usage: a count of AI actions per store, user, month and kind of action, used to apply your monthly allowance. It does not contain your photos or text.
  • Pick activity log: each screenshot read, sale recorded, list cleared and checklist shared, with your account's ID and email and the item codes involved.
  • eBay sale log: each order line detected on your eBay account, the item it matched and what the app did.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider and our functions keep technical logs (time, request type, errors, and AI call counts) to run and protect the service.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, store and show your catalog, print labels, compute your profit and keep your store separate from others.
  • To provide features you ask for: AI listings, narration, spreadsheet and screenshot reading, eBay publishing, delisting and sale tracking.
  • To keep the service safe and fair: verify email, check requests with Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise), apply AI allowances and investigate errors and abuse.
  • To contact you about your account or this policy.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your content to train our own models.

3. Who processes it for us

We use these providers. Each receives only what is needed for its job.

4. Storage on your device

The app keeps small pieces of data in your browser (local storage and similar) so it works the way you left it: your sign-in session (Firebase), your theme and catalog sort, the periods you chose on Home and Finances, rows you dismissed, and your Pick list in progress. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Google's App Check may set its own cookies or storage to do its check. You can clear all of this in your browser settings; you will be signed out.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your store's content stays until you delete it or ask us to delete the store.
  • Trash: an item you delete is kept in the trash for 30 days and then removed for good, or removed sooner if you choose Delete forever.
  • Pick activity log: kept while the store exists.
  • AI usage counts: kept while the store exists, to apply allowances and for our records.
  • eBay sale log: kept while the store exists.
  • Account: kept until you ask us to delete it.
  • Backups and logs kept by our providers are removed on their own schedules.

6. Your choices

  • You can edit or delete items, expenses and settings in the app, and disconnect eBay in Settings.
  • To get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account and store, email support@calculatedchaos.app from the address on the account. We may need to confirm it is you. We will answer within 45 days.
  • Residents of some states have additional rights over their personal information. We honor these requests for everyone who asks.

7. Buyers' information in your screenshots

If you upload a sale screenshot, you are choosing to send what it shows, which may include a buyer's name or address, to our AI provider so it can read the order. Please crop what you do not need. We use that information only to read the order and do not keep the image: it stays in your browser, is sent for the reading, and is not stored on our servers.

8. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, stores are separated by access rules, eBay tokens are kept only on the server, and we require a verified email. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and tell us right away if you think your account was used by someone else.

9. Children

The service is for adults running a resale business. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the version and date above. For a material change we will ask new stores to accept it again and tell existing ones in the app or by email.
